COMPANY HR POLICIES — SAMPLE DATA

Annual Leave:
Employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year.

Sick Leave:
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year.

Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

Working Hours:
Standard working hours are 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday through Friday.

Health Insurance:
Full-time employees are eligible for company-sponsored health insurance.

Leave Approval:
Employees must submit leave requests to their reporting manager.

Employee Benefits:
Benefits include health insurance, paid leave, and professional development support.
